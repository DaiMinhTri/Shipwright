# Shipwright

Maintained by DaiMinhTri (originally by RustyMods)

Shipwright hammer lets users repair ships on the go without the need of a crafting station.

Tired of having to swing your axe at your ship when you land on a new shores? Shipwright hammer can destroy your ship!

Scared those viking sailors might destroy your ship? Fear no more, for the shipwright hammer can repair at sea!

## Features
- Mouse0 to repair
- Mouse3 to destroy
- Configurable material cost (default is wood)
- Configurable repair and destroy duration
- Extra longship visuals can be enabled via configs
